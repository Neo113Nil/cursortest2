General

What is FoxtailSports?
FoxtailSports is an AI-powered sports analytics platform that provides game insights, team and player comparisons, and data-driven analytics across major sports leagues including NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, Golf, and soccer. Our tools help you make more informed decisions about sports outcomes.
Does FoxtailSports offer real-money wagering?
No. FoxtailSports does not offer any real-money wagering. We provide sports analytics and insights for entertainment and informational purposes only. What you do with our analytics is entirely up to you.
What sports do you cover?
We currently cover NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, Golf (PGA Tour and LPGA Tour), and major soccer leagues including the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, and MLS. We're continuously working to expand our coverage.
How accurate are your analytics?
Our AI models analyze thousands of data points to generate insights, but sports are inherently unpredictable. We provide confidence levels with our analytics to help you understand the strength of each analysis. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Using Custom AI Edge

How do I use AI Edge?
Step 1: Click upper or lower menu on Mobile (Click upper right icon on Web), then click on Edge Preferences.

Step 2: Choose Sport, choose Game, choose Model.

Step 3: You can choose to do analysis on Team or Players. Click on any stats that are important to you.

Step 4: Choose Offensive and Defensive stats - a continue button will appear below. When you choose Offense the system automatically switches the other team to Defense.

Step 5: Choose Prediction, click on the type of prediction you want to run, then click Generate Full AI Analysis.

You can save and automate your predictions for future use.
How do I save my automated AI Edge for later analysis?
When you are choosing your predictions you can click save and automate. That automation can be used in any predictions.

The system will automatically load your preferences for each league or team saved.

For example: If you analyze any NBA game, and you've saved your preferences it will automatically load key stats next time you go to any NBA game.
How do I see individual player stats per game and season?
In AI Edge you can click on View Games and you can see historical stats.

Props, Game Stats and Predictions

How do I see analysis of the games that Foxtail Sports has analyzed?
On any of the Hot Pick game cards or Regular game cards you can click on the Foxtail Analysis drop down. You can choose any of the following to see our analysis: Foxtail Edge, Value Edge, or Vegas Edge.
What is the difference between: Foxtail Edge, Value Edge and Vegas Edge?
Foxtail Edge: These are predictions made by our internal machine learning AI models.

Value Edge: These are predictions made leaning more towards parlays and prop bets.

Vegas Edge: These are predictions based on Vegas analysis, odds and lines which we feed into our internal machine learning AI models.
How do I see team match-ups to see who has the edge?
On any of the game cards click on the blue icon next to AI Edge. These are derived from season stats.
What is the Foxtail Confidence Score?
This is a score, out of 100, of the likelihood of a victory. This score is generated by our analysis using our AI and Machine Learning algorithms.
What are Hot Picks?
High confidence score games that have over a 75% chance of winning.
