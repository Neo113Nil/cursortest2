Account & Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your Foxtail Sports account and associated data

Delete Your Account

You can delete your Foxtail Sports account and all associated data at any time. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Self-Service Deletion (Recommended)

If you have an active account, you can delete it directly from your account settings:

  1. Log in to your Foxtail Sports account
  2. Go to your Account page
  3. Scroll to the bottom and click "Delete Account"
  4. Follow the confirmation steps

Option 2: Email Request

If you cannot access your account, you can request deletion by email:

Email: support@foxtailsports.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your account name (if you remember it)
  • Confirmation that you want to delete all data

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When you delete your account, we will permanently remove:

  • Your account profile (name, email, password)
  • All saved predictions and sports preferences
  • AI chat history and conversations
  • Subscription and purchase history
  • Favorite teams and leagues
  • All other personal data associated with your account

Data Retention & Timeline

Deletion Timeline: Your data will be deleted within 30 days of your request.

Retained Data: Some data may be retained for legal, security, or operational purposes, including:

  • Transaction records (required for tax and accounting purposes)
  • Logs for security and fraud prevention
  • Data required by law to be retained

Retained data is kept secure and will be deleted as soon as legally permissible.

Important: Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. You must cancel separately:

  • Stripe subscriptions: Cancel via your account page before deletion
  • Google Play subscriptions: Cancel via Google Play Store app
  • Apple subscriptions: Cancel via App Store settings

Questions or Issues?

If you have questions about data deletion or encounter any issues, please contact us:

Email: support@foxtailsports.com

Privacy Policy: View our Privacy Policy
