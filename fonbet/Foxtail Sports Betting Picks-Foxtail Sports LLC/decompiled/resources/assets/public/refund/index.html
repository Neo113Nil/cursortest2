Refund Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

Overview

At Foxtail Sports, we want you to be completely satisfied with your subscription. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued for our subscription services and credit purchases.

Free Trial

We offer a 30-day free trial for new users. During this period, you can explore all features without any charge. No payment information is required to start your free trial, so there is nothing to refund if you decide not to continue.

Subscription Refunds

Because we offer a comprehensive 30-day free trial that provides full access to our platform without requiring payment information, subscription purchases are generally non-refundable. By the time you subscribe, you have had ample opportunity to evaluate our Services.

However, we may consider refund requests on a case-by-case basis for extenuating circumstances such as:

  • Technical issues that prevented you from using the Service
  • Billing errors or unauthorized charges
  • Service unavailability for extended periods
  • Duplicate charges

AI Credit Purchases

Purchased AI credits are generally non-refundable once added to your account. However, we may issue refunds for credit purchases if:

  • The request is made within 24 hours of purchase
  • None of the purchased credits have been used
  • There was a billing error or technical issue

Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings or the Stripe customer portal. When you cancel:

  • Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period
  • You will not be charged for future billing periods
  • No partial refunds are issued for unused time in the current period
  • Unused monthly credits will expire at the end of the billing period

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team with:

  • Your account email address
  • Date of the charge
  • Reason for the refund request
  • Any relevant details or documentation

Email us at support@foxtailsports.com with the subject line "Refund Request".

Processing Time

Refund requests are typically reviewed within 2-3 business days. If approved, refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or credit card company.

Exceptions

Refunds will not be issued in cases of:

  • Violation of our Terms & Conditions
  • Account suspension due to abuse or fraud
  • Dissatisfaction with analytics accuracy (analytics are for entertainment purposes)
  • Failure to cancel before a renewal date

Contact Us

If you have questions about our Refund Policy or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out:

Email: support@foxtailsports.com

You can also visit our Contact Page to submit a support request.
