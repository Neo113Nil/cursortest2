FoxtailSports – Our Commitment to Privacy

FoxtailSports LLC ("FoxtailSports," "we," "us," or "our") provides sports data, analytics, and AI-powered tools to help users better understand and analyze outcomes of sports events. We do not offer real-money wagering on our Website or Applications.

To operate our Services and improve your experience, we collect and process information about our users. We are committed to handling your information responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use:

  • our website at https://www.foxtailsports.com (the "Website");
  • our mobile applications, if any (the "Applications"); and
  • any related tools, content, predictive models, analytics, social and community features, and other services we provide (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Notice does not apply to websites, applications, or services that do not display or link to it, or that display or link to a different privacy statement.

By using our Services, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Notice. If you do not agree, please do not use our Services.

If you are a California resident, see Section 12 for additional information about your privacy rights, including how to opt out of certain data sharing.

If you are a resident of Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Texas, Utah, Virginia, or another U.S. state with a consumer privacy law, see Section 13.

If you are physically located in Canada, see Section 14.

1. The Personal Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide To Us

You may voluntarily provide, or direct third parties to provide, certain personal information to us. "Personal information" means information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked to you, such as your name, address, email address, phone number, payment details, and online identifiers.

Examples include:

  • Account registration: When you create an account ("Account") to use our Services, you may provide your name, username, password, email address, phone number, date of birth, and country/state of residence. We use this information to create and manage your Account, provide you access to our AI tools and analytics, and communicate with you.
  • Purchases and subscriptions: If you purchase a subscription or paid offering, we (or our payment processors) may collect your name, billing address, payment card or other financial account information, and records of the products or Services you purchase, obtain, or consider.
  • Customer support: If you contact us by email, chat, phone, or text message, we may collect your name, contact details, and the contents of your message so we can respond, provide support, improve our Services, and address legal or security issues. We and our service providers may record or monitor communications as permitted by law. By communicating with us, you consent to such recording and monitoring.
  • Profile information: You may optionally upload a profile photo or display name, or provide additional profile details that may be seen by others if you participate in community or social features.
  • Location (if you choose to share): If you allow your device to share location information, we may collect and use that information to personalize content, help prevent fraud, and comply with applicable laws.
  • Surveys and feedback: If you take part in surveys or provide feedback, we may collect additional information about your preferences, interests, and how you use our Services in order to improve them.
  • Promotions and giveaways: If we run a sweepstakes, giveaway, or promotion, we may collect your name, email, phone number, username, and other details necessary to administer the promotion, verify eligibility, and deliver any prizes.

b. Information Collected Via Technology

When you use the Website, Applications, or other Services, we may automatically collect certain technical and usage information, such as:

  • browser type and settings;
  • operating system;
  • IP address;
  • device identifiers;
  • pages viewed and links clicked;
  • referring and exit pages;
  • date and time of visits;
  • general location (derived from IP or other signals); and
  • usage patterns related to our sports data tools and analytics features.

We use this information to operate and administer the Services, analyze use and improve performance, protect our rights and the security of our systems, detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access, and develop new features and AI tools.

Device Information

We may collect information about the devices you use to access the Services, including hardware model, operating system and version, device settings, unique device identifiers, network information, and crash or performance data.

Cookies

We use "cookies" and similar technologies to collect certain information when you use the Services. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember you when you return, understand how you use the Services, personalize content, and measure and improve performance.

We may use both session cookies (which expire when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (which remain until deleted or they expire). You can manage cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Some features of the Services may not function correctly if certain cookies are disabled.

Pixel Tags and Similar Technologies

We may use pixel tags (also known as clear GIFs, web beacons, or web bugs) in our Website, Applications, or emails to help us understand how users interact with our pages and emails, determine whether emails have been opened, and measure the effectiveness of our communications.

Threat Detection and Security

We may use third-party security and fraud-prevention services to help protect our Services and users. These services may collect information such as IP address, device identifiers, and usage patterns to detect suspicious or malicious activity.

Advertising and Marketing Data

We may work with advertising and analytics partners that use cookies, pixel tags, and similar technologies to understand how users find and use our Services, measure the performance of marketing campaigns, and deliver or measure advertisements for our Services on other sites and apps.

For more information about online advertising choices, you can visit http://www.aboutads.info/choices or https://www.networkadvertising.org/.

c. Information Collected From or About Friends

If we offer a refer-a-friend or similar program, we may provide you with a unique referral link that you can share. If someone you invite signs up using your link, we may associate that new Account with you for purposes such as crediting referral rewards.

If you choose to use tools that allow us to access your contacts, we may collect your contacts' names, email addresses, and phone numbers to send invitations and help them connect with you. You are responsible for obtaining any consents required by law from your contacts before sharing their information with us.

d. Information Collected From Other Sources

We may supplement information we collect from you with information obtained from other sources, such as service providers and vendors, analytics and advertising partners, social networks, and data brokers or public databases.
